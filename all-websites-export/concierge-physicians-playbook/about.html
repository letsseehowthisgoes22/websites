About This Publication

Editorial Mission

The Concierge Physician's Playbook is an independent professional resource for private and concierge physicians whose practices intersect with behavioral health. Our content addresses the clinical, logistical, and relational challenges that arise when a private physician's patient — or a patient's family member — is affected by a substance use disorder or other behavioral health condition.

Why This Exists

Concierge physicians occupy a unique position in healthcare. They enjoy an unusual degree of access to and trust from their patients. They see patients longitudinally, over years and decades. And they frequently serve as the first point of contact when a health crisis — including a behavioral health crisis — emerges. Yet most physician training programs devote minimal time to addiction medicine, and the specific challenges of managing behavioral health in UHNW populations are addressed nowhere in standard medical education.

This publication addresses that gap with practical, evidence-based content written for practicing physicians. Our articles draw on addiction medicine literature, clinical experience, and the practical realities of concierge practice to provide guidance that is both clinically rigorous and immediately applicable.

Editorial Independence

Our content is editorially independent. We do not accept payment for coverage or inclusion in our directory. When we reference specific programs, providers, or organizations, we do so based on editorial assessment of their relevance and quality, not commercial arrangement. Our recommendations reflect current evidence and professional practice standards.

Intended Audience

This publication is written for licensed physicians practicing concierge, direct-pay, or private medicine. While our content may be of interest to other healthcare professionals, it assumes a physician's clinical training and perspective. Nothing on this site constitutes medical advice or establishes a physician-patient relationship.

Contact

Physicians with editorial inquiries, content suggestions, or corrections may reach us at editor@conciergereferral.com.

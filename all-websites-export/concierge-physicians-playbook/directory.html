Directory of Concierge Medical Practices

Professional Resource · Updated January 2026

The following directory identifies concierge and private medical practices, behavioral health organizations, and professional resources relevant to physicians serving UHNW patients with behavioral health needs. Inclusion reflects editorial assessment of relevance and does not constitute endorsement. Physicians should conduct independent evaluation before establishing referral relationships.

National Concierge Medicine Networks

MD2

MD2 operates a highly exclusive concierge medicine model, limiting each physician's practice to approximately 50 families. Founded in Seattle, the firm maintains practices in major metropolitan areas. The extremely low patient-to-physician ratio enables the kind of longitudinal relationship and availability that is particularly valuable in managing behavioral health situations. MD2 physicians are positioned as the primary health coordinator for their patients, including behavioral health referrals.

MDVIP

MDVIP is the largest concierge medicine network in the United States, with over 1,100 affiliated physicians. MDVIP practices limit patient panels to approximately 600 patients per physician, allowing for longer appointments and same-day or next-day access. While MDVIP's scale means that behavioral health expertise varies across its network, the organization provides practice support resources and continuing education that may include behavioral health topics.

Sollis Health

Sollis Health operates a membership-based urgent and primary care model with locations in New York, Los Angeles, the Hamptons, and South Florida. The model combines 24/7 urgent care access with primary care coordination, which can be valuable during behavioral health crises that require immediate medical evaluation. Sollis maintains relationships with specialty providers, including behavioral health professionals, for member referrals.

Behavioral Health Specialists

Addiction Psychiatry

Physicians seeking addiction psychiatry referrals for UHNW patients should look for practitioners with board certification in addiction psychiatry through the American Board of Psychiatry and Neurology, or addiction medicine certification through the American Board of Preventive Medicine. The American Academy of Addiction Psychiatry maintains a member directory. Private-practice addiction psychiatrists who accept private-pay patients and offer extended appointments are relatively rare; building a relationship with one or two before a crisis arises is essential.

Care Management Firms

O'Connor Professional Group provides behavioral health care management to UHNW families, serving as a clinical intermediary between families, treatment providers, and professional advisors. The firm coordinates treatment placement, monitors progress, and advises on long-term care strategy. Similar firms operating in this space include Concierge Care Collective, which provides comprehensive case management and in-home clinical support in New York, Palm Beach, Beverly Hills, and other major markets.

Recovery Support Providers

Professional sober companion and recovery coaching services appropriate for UHNW clients require a combination of clinical credential, experiential recovery knowledge, and the social sophistication to operate in high-net-worth environments. Firms providing these services include Coast Health Consulting, SilverBell Global (which operates an international network of recovery professionals), Spearhead Health in Beverly Hills, and Hired Power in Southern California. The International Association of Professional Recovery Coaches provides certification and a provider directory.

Professional Organizations

  • American Academy of Addiction Psychiatry (AAAP): Professional organization for addiction psychiatrists. Maintains a member directory and publishes the American Journal on Addictions.
  • American Society of Addiction Medicine (ASAM): Multidisciplinary organization of physicians and associated professionals. Publisher of the ASAM Criteria and the Journal of Addiction Medicine.
  • American Academy of Private Physicians (AAPP): Professional organization for concierge and direct-pay physicians. Provides practice resources and advocacy for the concierge medicine model.
  • Concierge Medicine Today: Industry publication covering trends, regulatory developments, and practice management in concierge medicine.

Continuing Education Resources

  • ASAM Annual Conference: The premier continuing education event for addiction medicine, featuring clinical updates, research presentations, and networking opportunities.
  • American Psychiatric Association (APA) Annual Meeting: Includes tracks on addiction psychiatry and co-occurring disorders.
  • NIDA Clinical Trials Network: Provides access to current research on addiction treatment methodologies and pharmacotherapies.
  • SAMHSA Training and Technical Assistance: Free continuing education resources on substance use disorder screening, brief intervention, and referral to treatment (SBIRT).

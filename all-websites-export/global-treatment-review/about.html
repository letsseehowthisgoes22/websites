About This Publication

Editorial Mission

The Global Addiction Treatment Review is an independent editorial publication providing profiles and analysis of addiction treatment programs worldwide. We write for families, professional advisors, and healthcare providers who need reliable, comparative information about treatment options across jurisdictions.

Why This Exists

The global treatment landscape is vast, fragmented, and poorly documented. Families facing the decision of where to send a loved one for treatment must navigate a marketplace in which marketing claims are abundant, independent evaluation is scarce, and the stakes are as high as they can be. This publication exists to provide a starting point for that evaluation — honest, detailed profiles that describe what programs actually do, what standards they meet, and how they compare to alternatives.

Editorial Independence

This publication is editorially independent. We do not accept payment for coverage, and our inclusion of any program in our profiles reflects editorial judgment. No program can purchase coverage, influence the content of its profile, or preview our assessment before publication. Our goal is to serve families and professional advisors with information that is genuinely useful, factually accurate, and free from commercial influence.

Intended Audience

Our content is written for families evaluating treatment options for a loved one, care managers and clinical consultants who advise families on treatment placement, concierge physicians who refer patients to treatment programs, family office professionals who manage the logistics of treatment, and attorneys and fiduciary advisors who may be involved in treatment decisions.

Contact

Readers with editorial inquiries, content suggestions, corrections, or program update information may reach us at editor@globaladdictionguide.com.

About This Publication

The Modern Interventionist exists to advance the professionalization of the intervention field through rigorous, evidence-based content for practicing and aspiring intervention professionals.

Our Purpose

The intervention field has evolved dramatically from its confrontational origins, but the literature and resources available to practitioners have not kept pace. Most published material on intervention is either consumer-oriented (written for families considering intervention) or dated (reflecting methodologies that have been superseded by evidence-based approaches). There is a gap in the professional literature for content that speaks directly to practicing interventionists about methodology, ethics, professional development, and the practical challenges of crisis work.

This publication addresses that gap. Our articles are written for intervention professionals who want to deepen their clinical sophistication, stay current with evolving methodologies, build sustainable practices, and contribute to the professionalization of their field.

Editorial Standards

Our content is grounded in published research, clinical evidence, and the collective experience of practicing intervention professionals. When we reference specific intervention models, we cite the primary research. When we discuss practice challenges, we draw on real (anonymized) scenarios. When we evaluate certifications or training programs, we assess them on their evidence base, rigor, and professional recognition — not on their marketing.

Editorial Independence

This publication is editorially independent. We do not accept payment for coverage, and our inclusion of any program, provider, or organization in our directory or articles reflects editorial judgment. Our goal is to serve the intervention profession with content that is genuinely useful, factually accurate, and free from commercial influence.

Contact

Intervention professionals with editorial inquiries, content suggestions, or corrections may reach our editorial team at editor@interventionist.co.

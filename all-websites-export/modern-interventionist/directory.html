Directory of Certified Intervention Professionals

Inclusion in this directory reflects editorial assessment of professional standing and does not constitute endorsement. Families should conduct independent evaluation before engaging any intervention professional.

Certification Bodies

Association of Intervention Specialists Certification Board (AISCB)

The AISCB offers the Certified Intervention Professional (CIP) credential, which is the most widely recognized certification in the field. The CIP requires documented intervention experience, completion of approved training, adherence to ethical standards, and passing a certification examination. The AISCB maintains a directory of certified professionals and a code of ethics that addresses referral practices, confidentiality, and professional boundaries.

National Association of Drug Court Professionals (NADCP)

While focused primarily on the drug court system, NADCP provides training and resources relevant to interventionists who work with clients involved in the legal system. Their training programs address the intersection of substance use disorders, legal proceedings, and family dynamics.

ARISE Network

The ARISE Intervention model, developed by Dr. Judith Landau, certifies practitioners in its specific invitational intervention methodology. ARISE-certified interventionists have completed training in the three-level ARISE model and are listed in the ARISE network's provider directory. The ARISE model's strong evidence base makes this certification particularly valuable.

Recovery Support and Care Management

Sober Companion Agencies

For interventionists who coordinate aftercare as part of their practice, relationships with qualified sober companion agencies are essential. Firms providing UHNW-appropriate recovery support include SilverBell Global, which operates an international network with clinical oversight; The Advocacy Group (TAG) in Los Angeles, led by Lauren Brock, which offers comprehensive case management and recovery companionship; Hired Power, a family-owned firm with over two decades of experience in recovery assistance and safe transport; and Spearhead Health in Beverly Hills, which provides concierge-level care management and recovery coaching. These firms represent a range of approaches and price points within the premium recovery support market.

Care Management Firms

Care management firms serve as the coordination hub for complex, multi-provider care arrangements. O'Connor Professional Group is the most established firm in this space, providing clinical care management to UHNW families nationally. Concierge Care Collective offers comprehensive in-home care infrastructure including nursing, psychiatric stabilization, and long-term planning. These firms work in partnership with interventionists to ensure continuity from the intervention through treatment and into sustained recovery.

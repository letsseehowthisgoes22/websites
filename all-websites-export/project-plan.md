# Adolescent Health Websites Project Plan

## Website Topics & Focus Areas

### Site 1: Adolescent Crisis Intervention Hub
- **Focus**: Crisis intervention, emergency resources, immediate support
- **Platform**: Vercel
- **Primary Regions**: California, Texas, NYC
- **Key Topics**: Crisis hotlines, emergency protocols, family crisis management

### Site 2: Youth Mental Health Resources Network  
- **Focus**: Mental health resources, therapy options, family support
- **Platform**: Netlify
- **Primary Regions**: UK, Florida, Tennessee
- **Key Topics**: Therapy access, mental health awareness, family guidance

### Site 3: Family Transport & Behavioral Health Guide
- **Focus**: Safe transport services, behavioral health programs
- **Platform**: GitHub Pages
- **Primary Regions**: Utah, San Francisco, California
- **Key Topics**: Transport safety, program selection, family preparation

### Site 4: Adolescent Behavioral Health Support Center
- **Focus**: Behavioral interventions, educational resources, parent support
- **Platform**: Railway
- **Primary Regions**: UK, New York, Texas
- **Key Topics**: Behavioral strategies, educational support, family counseling

### Site 5: Teen Crisis & Family Support Portal
- **Focus**: Crisis prevention, family dynamics, support systems
- **Platform**: Render
- **Primary Regions**: Florida, Tennessee, Utah
- **Key Topics**: Prevention strategies, family therapy, support networks

### Site 6: Youth Wellness & Intervention Resources
- **Focus**: Wellness programs, early intervention, community resources
- **Platform**: Surge.sh
- **Primary Regions**: California, San Francisco, NYC
- **Key Topics**: Wellness programs, community support, early intervention

## Content Strategy
- Each article 800-1500 words
- Region-specific resources and regulations
- Credible source citations (.gov, .edu, medical authorities)
- Strategic backlinks to main site (contextual, not over-optimized)
- E-E-A-T compliant content

## Technical Requirements
- Unique designs for each site
- Google Analytics integration
- Google Search Console submission
- Mobile-responsive layouts
- Fast loading times
- SEO optimization

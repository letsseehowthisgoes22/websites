🚨 Emergency Situations

If you or your teenager is in immediate danger, please call 911 or go to your nearest emergency room immediately.

For crisis support: Call 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or text "HELLO" to 741741 (Crisis Text Line)

About This Resource

The Adolescent Crisis Intervention Hub is an educational resource designed to provide families with comprehensive information about crisis intervention services, resources, and support systems for adolescents experiencing behavioral health challenges.

Please note: This website provides educational information only and does not offer direct crisis intervention services, counseling, or medical advice. All content is for informational purposes and should not replace professional medical, psychological, or legal consultation.

Educational Purpose

Our mission is to provide evidence-based information to help families understand:

  • Crisis intervention processes and procedures
  • Regional resources and support systems
  • Legal considerations and family rights
  • Best practices for family crisis management
  • Professional treatment options and considerations

Content Standards

All information provided on this site is:

  • Reviewed by licensed mental health professionals
  • Based on current research and best practices
  • Updated regularly to reflect policy changes
  • Designed to complement professional care
  • Culturally sensitive and inclusive

Feedback & Suggestions

We welcome feedback about our resources and suggestions for additional content that would benefit families navigating adolescent behavioral health challenges.

While we cannot provide direct crisis support or individual consultation, we value input that helps us improve our educational resources.

Professional Resources

For direct support and intervention services, please contact:

  • Your local crisis intervention center
  • Your teenager's healthcare provider
  • Licensed mental health professionals
  • Your county mental health department
  • School counselors and support staff

Disclaimer

The information provided on this website is for educational purposes only and is not intended as a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always seek the advice of qualified healthcare providers with questions regarding adolescent behavioral health concerns.

In crisis situations, immediate professional intervention may be necessary. This website cannot provide emergency services or immediate crisis intervention.

Regional Resource Accuracy

While we strive to maintain accurate and current information about regional resources, crisis services, and legal frameworks, these can change frequently. We recommend verifying current information directly with local providers and agencies.

The inclusion of external links and resources does not constitute an endorsement of those services, and families should conduct their own research when selecting professional services.

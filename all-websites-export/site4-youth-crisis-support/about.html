About Youth Crisis Support Network

Dedicated to providing comprehensive crisis intervention resources and support for adolescents and families

Our Mission

Empowering Families Through Crisis

Youth Crisis Support Network exists to provide comprehensive, accessible, and culturally competent crisis intervention resources for adolescents and families facing mental health emergencies. We believe that every young person deserves immediate access to quality crisis support, regardless of their location, background, or circumstances.

Founded on the principle that crisis intervention should be both immediate and compassionate, our network serves as a comprehensive resource hub connecting families to vital crisis services across multiple regions. We understand that mental health crises can occur at any time and in any place, which is why we've developed region-specific resources that address the unique needs and available services in different communities.

Our platform serves families, caregivers, mental health professionals, educators, and community members who need reliable information about crisis intervention services. We recognize that navigating crisis situations can be overwhelming, and our goal is to provide clear, actionable information that can help save lives and connect families to appropriate support.

What We Provide

  • Comprehensive Crisis Resources: Detailed information about crisis intervention strategies, emergency response protocols, and suicide prevention approaches
  • Regional Service Directories: Location-specific guides to crisis services in the UK, California, Utah, Tennessee, New York City, San Francisco, Florida, and Texas
  • Educational Content: In-depth articles about crisis prevention, family recovery, mobile crisis services, and school-based crisis protocols
  • Emergency Contact Information: Quick access to crisis hotlines, text services, and emergency resources
  • Family Support Guidance: Resources specifically designed to help families navigate crisis situations and support their loved ones

Our Approach

We take a comprehensive, evidence-based approach to crisis intervention information, drawing from established mental health organizations, government resources, and clinical best practices. Our content is developed with input from mental health professionals and is regularly updated to reflect current standards of care and available services.

Every resource on our platform is designed with the understanding that crisis situations require immediate, accurate information. We prioritize clarity, accessibility, and cultural competency in all our content, ensuring that families from diverse backgrounds can find relevant, appropriate support.

Our Values

Core Principles

  • Accessibility: Crisis resources should be available to everyone, regardless of geographic location or economic circumstances
  • Cultural Competency: Crisis services must be appropriate and respectful of diverse cultural backgrounds and communities
  • Family-Centered Care: Effective crisis intervention involves and supports entire families, not just the individual in crisis
  • Evidence-Based Practice: All recommendations are grounded in established research and clinical best practices
  • Hope and Recovery: We believe in the possibility of healing and recovery, even in the most challenging circumstances

Commitment to Quality

We are committed to maintaining the highest standards of accuracy and reliability in all our content. Our resources are regularly reviewed and updated to ensure they reflect current best practices, available services, and contact information. We work closely with established mental health organizations and crisis service providers to verify the accuracy of our information.

Our team understands the critical importance of accurate crisis information. We take seriously our responsibility to provide resources that can genuinely help families in their most vulnerable moments. Every piece of content is carefully researched, fact-checked, and designed to provide practical, actionable guidance.

Community Partnership

We believe that effective crisis intervention requires strong community partnerships. While we provide information and resources, we recognize that actual crisis services are delivered by dedicated professionals in hospitals, community mental health centers, schools, and specialized crisis programs.

Our role is to bridge the gap between families in crisis and the services they need. We work to highlight the excellent work being done by crisis intervention professionals and organizations across different regions, helping families understand what services are available and how to access them.

Important Disclaimers

Educational Resource Only

This website is for informational and educational purposes only. The content provided on Youth Crisis Support Network is not intended to replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always seek the advice of qualified mental health professionals or medical providers with any questions you may have regarding a mental health condition or crisis situation.

Emergency Situations

If you or someone you know is in immediate danger or having thoughts of suicide, please contact emergency services immediately:

  • Call 911 for immediate emergency assistance
  • Call 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline
  • Text HOME to 741741 for the Crisis Text Line
  • Go to your nearest emergency room for immediate evaluation

Information Accuracy

While we strive to provide accurate and up-to-date information, crisis services, contact information, and available resources can change. We encourage users to verify current information directly with service providers. We are not responsible for any changes in services or contact information that may occur after publication.

The inclusion of specific organizations, services, or resources on our website does not constitute an endorsement. We provide information about available services to help families make informed decisions about their care, but we do not recommend specific providers over others.

Privacy and Confidentiality

We respect the privacy of all visitors to our website. We do not collect personal information about individuals in crisis, nor do we provide direct crisis intervention services. For actual crisis support, please contact the appropriate crisis services in your area.

If you are seeking crisis intervention services, please be aware that different organizations have different privacy policies and confidentiality practices. We encourage you to ask about privacy policies when contacting crisis services directly.

Contact Information

Youth Crisis Support Network is a resource platform dedicated to connecting families with crisis intervention services. We are not a crisis intervention service provider ourselves, but rather an information hub designed to help families find appropriate support.

For Crisis Support

If you are experiencing a mental health crisis, please contact crisis services directly rather than contacting us. We provide information about crisis services but do not provide direct crisis intervention.

Resource Updates

If you are a crisis service provider and notice that information about your services needs to be updated, or if you are aware of additional crisis resources that should be included in our regional guides, we welcome your input to help us maintain accurate, comprehensive information.

Our commitment is to serve as a reliable bridge between families in crisis and the professional services that can help them. We are honored to play this role in supporting youth and families during their most challenging moments.

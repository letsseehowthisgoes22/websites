Important Notice

We Are Not a Crisis Service Provider

Youth Crisis Support Network is an educational resource platform that provides information about crisis intervention services. We do not provide direct crisis intervention, counseling, or emergency response services. If you are experiencing a mental health crisis, please contact professional crisis services immediately using the emergency contacts above.

Our role is to serve as an information hub that connects families, caregivers, and community members with comprehensive resources about crisis intervention services. We provide educational content, regional service directories, and guidance to help people understand available crisis support options in their communities.

What We Do

  • Educational Resources: Provide comprehensive information about crisis intervention strategies and approaches
  • Service Directories: Maintain region-specific guides to crisis services and resources
  • Resource Updates: Regularly update information about available crisis services and contact information
  • Community Information: Share information about crisis prevention, family support, and recovery resources

What We Don't Do

  • Provide direct crisis intervention or counseling services
  • Offer medical or mental health advice
  • Respond to crisis situations or emergency calls
  • Replace professional mental health treatment
  • Provide referrals to specific providers (we provide general information about available services)

Resource Updates and Feedback

Help Us Maintain Accurate Information

We are committed to providing accurate, up-to-date information about crisis services. If you notice outdated information, missing resources, or have suggestions for improving our content, we welcome your input to help us better serve families and communities.

Types of Updates We Welcome

  • Service Information Changes: Updates to crisis service contact information, hours, or availability
  • New Resources: Information about new crisis services or programs in covered regions
  • Content Corrections: Corrections to factual information in our educational content
  • Regional Additions: Information about crisis services in areas we cover
  • Accessibility Improvements: Suggestions for making our resources more accessible

Information We Need

When providing updates or feedback, please include:

  • Specific page or resource that needs updating
  • Current incorrect information (if applicable)
  • Correct or updated information
  • Source or verification for the updated information
  • Your role or connection to the service (if applicable)

Response Time

We review all feedback and update requests regularly. While we cannot provide immediate responses to individual messages, we prioritize updates that affect the accuracy of crisis service information, as we understand the critical importance of accurate emergency contact information.

For Crisis Service Providers

If you are a crisis service provider, mental health organization, or healthcare system and would like to ensure that information about your services is accurate and current, we welcome your input.

Service Provider Information

We include information about crisis services based on publicly available information and established service directories. If you would like to:

  • Update information about your crisis services
  • Correct any inaccuracies in how your services are described
  • Provide additional information about specialized programs
  • Update contact information or service hours

Please provide detailed information about your services, including verification of your role with the organization. We verify all service provider information before making updates to ensure accuracy.

Service Inclusion Criteria

We include information about crisis services that:

  • Provide direct crisis intervention services
  • Are accessible to the general public
  • Serve the geographic regions covered by our resource guides
  • Have established, verifiable contact information
  • Meet professional standards for crisis intervention

Privacy and Confidentiality

Important Privacy Notice

Do not include personal information, details about specific crisis situations, or confidential information in any communications with us. We are not equipped to handle personal crisis information and cannot provide confidential support.

Information We Collect

We may collect basic information necessary to process feedback and resource updates, such as:

  • Contact information for follow-up questions about resource updates
  • Professional affiliation (for service provider updates)
  • General location information (for regional resource updates)

Information We Don't Collect

  • Personal crisis information or mental health details
  • Information about specific individuals in crisis
  • Confidential medical or treatment information
  • Personal identifying information beyond what's necessary for resource updates

Data Use

Any information provided to us is used solely for the purpose of maintaining accurate crisis resource information. We do not share personal information with third parties, use information for marketing purposes, or maintain databases of personal information.

Disclaimer and Limitations

Educational Purpose Only: All content on Youth Crisis Support Network is for educational and informational purposes only. This information is not intended to replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

No Professional Relationship: Use of this website does not create a professional relationship between users and Youth Crisis Support Network. We do not provide professional services, counseling, or medical advice.

Information Accuracy: While we strive to maintain accurate information, crisis services and contact information can change. We encourage users to verify current information directly with service providers.

Emergency Situations: This website is not monitored for emergency situations. If you are experiencing a crisis, contact emergency services immediately rather than using this website.

Our Commitment

We are committed to serving as a reliable information resource for families and communities seeking crisis intervention services. Our goal is to provide accurate, comprehensive information that can help connect people with the professional services they need during difficult times.

We take seriously our responsibility to maintain accurate crisis resource information and welcome feedback that helps us fulfill this mission effectively.

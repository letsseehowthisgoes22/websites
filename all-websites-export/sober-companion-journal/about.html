About This Journal

Editorial Mission

The Sober Companion Journal is an independent professional publication dedicated to advancing standards, practice, and professional development in the recovery support field. We write for practitioners — sober companions, recovery coaches, and allied professionals — who are committed to clinical excellence and the professionalization of their craft.

Why This Exists

The sober companion field has grown significantly, but its professional literature has not kept pace. Practitioners who want to deepen their clinical knowledge, stay current with evolving best practices, or engage with the professional and ethical questions facing their field have limited resources available to them. This journal addresses that gap with rigorous, evidence-informed content written specifically for recovery support professionals.

Editorial Standards

Our articles are grounded in published research, clinical evidence, and the practical realities of recovery support practice. When we reference clinical frameworks, we cite the primary research. When we analyze the competitive landscape, we base our analysis on verifiable information. When we propose professional standards, we ground our proposals in existing certification requirements and emerging best practices.

Editorial Independence

This journal is editorially independent. We do not accept payment for coverage, and our inclusion of any firm, practitioner, or organization reflects editorial judgment. We are committed to honest, balanced analysis that serves the profession rather than any commercial interest.

Contact

Recovery support professionals with editorial inquiries, content suggestions, or corrections may reach us at editor@soberanywhere.com.

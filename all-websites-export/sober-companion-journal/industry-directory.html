Industry Directory

Professional Resource · Updated January 2026

Inclusion in this directory reflects editorial assessment of industry relevance. It does not constitute endorsement of any provider, organization, or program. Practitioners and families should conduct independent evaluation before engaging services.

Sober Companion and Recovery Support Agencies

SilverBell Global — International network of recovery professionals. Centralized clinical oversight. Global deployment capability.

The Advocacy Group (TAG) — Los Angeles. Led by Lauren Brock. Comprehensive case management and recovery companionship. Entertainment industry specialization.

Hired Power — Southern California. Family-owned, 20+ years of operation. Safe transport, sober companion placement, recovery assistance.

ALYST Health — Los Angeles. Joint Commission-accredited at-home addiction treatment. Clinical treatment model incorporating recovery support.

Spearhead Health — Beverly Hills. Concierge care management and recovery coaching. Lifestyle integration approach.

Concierge Care Collective — New York, Palm Beach, Beverly Hills, Aspen. Comprehensive in-home care including nursing, psychiatric stabilization, and recovery support.

Coast Health Consulting — Sober companion placement and care coordination. Emphasis on credential verification and companion-client matching.

Active Recovery Companions — Sober companion services with emphasis on active lifestyle integration and experiential recovery support.

Sober Services International — International sober companion and recovery support services.

A Sober Companion — Sober companion placement services.

Connections in Recovery — Recovery coaching and sober companion services.

James Haggerty Recovery — Independent practitioner. Long-standing reputation in UHNW recovery support.

Synergy Health Beverly Hills — Beverly Hills-based wellness and recovery support services.

Certification Bodies

Connecticut Community for Addiction Recovery (CCAR) — Offers the Certified Addiction Recovery Coach (CARC) credential. Recovery Coach Academy training program.

International Association of Professional Recovery Coaches (IAPRC) — Offers the Certified Professional Recovery Coach (CPRC) credential.

Pennsylvania Certification Board (PCB) — Administers the Certified Recovery Specialist (CRS) and other recovery support credentials recognized in multiple states.

National Association for Alcoholism and Drug Abuse Counselors (NAADAC) — Offers the National Certified Peer Recovery Support Specialist (NCPRSS) credential.

Care Management Firms

O'Connor Professional Group — Clinical care management for UHNW families. National scope. The most established firm in behavioral health care management.

Professional Development Resources

CCAR Recovery Coach Academy — Foundational training in recovery coaching principles, ethics, and practice. Offered in multiple formats including in-person and virtual.

Motivational Interviewing Network of Trainers (MINT) — Directory of certified MI trainers offering workshops at various levels.

SAMHSA's Bringing Recovery Supports to Scale Technical Assistance Center Strategy (BRSS TACS) — Resources for recovery support services development and implementation.

Faces & Voices of Recovery — National advocacy organization promoting recovery-oriented systems of care. Training and technical assistance resources.

Research and Publications

Journal of Substance Abuse Treatment — Peer-reviewed journal publishing research on treatment and recovery support efficacy.

Alcoholism Treatment Quarterly — Peer-reviewed journal covering current practices in addiction treatment.

William White Papers — Extensive library of historical and contemporary writings on addiction recovery by William L. White, including foundational work on recovery capital and the professionalization of recovery support.
